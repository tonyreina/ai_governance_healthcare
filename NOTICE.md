## About CHAI content

This project is an independent implementation. It is not affiliated with,
endorsed by, or certified by the Coalition for Health AI (CHAI).

- The six-stage lifecycle (define, design, engineer, assess, pilot, deploy and
  monitor) and the five principles are modeled on CHAI's published lifecycle
  approach; we could not retrieve CHAI's Assurance Standards Guide to check them
  against it. The four checkpoints, their placement (after stages 1, 4, 5 and
  6) and their questions are this project's rendering of CHAI's go/no-go
  decision points, not a structure taken from CHAI.
- The checklist criteria in this tool are original, paraphrased summaries written
  for this project. They are **not** the text of CHAI's Responsible AI Checklist.
- CHAI's use-case **Testing & Evaluation Frameworks** are published under
  **CC BY 4.0**, which permits reproduction with attribution. Their metric
  names, benchmarks and descriptions ARE reproduced in this tool and on
  [CHAI metrics](docs/frameworks/chai-metrics.md), attributed to CHAI.
- The model card uses the section and field names of the CHAI Applied Model Card
  template (Draft Nov 2024, v0.1). CHAI's own files do not state one license for
  it. As checked on 2026-10-12: the v0.1 documentation PDF
  (mc.chai.org/v0.1/documentation.pdf) links CC BY-ND 4.0; CHAI's `mc-schema`
  repository carries a different, permissive license that allows modification
  and derivative works; CHAI's registry page says "All rights reserved"; and a
  standalone template PDF was reported to carry CC BY-NC-ND 4.0, which we could
  not confirm because the file is gone. We have not resolved which governs. This
  tool reproduces the template's section and field names to structure its own
  output rather than redistributing CHAI's document, and the model card produced
  is this project's own artifact. Before you distribute a modified model card or
  something closer to CHAI's template, confirm the terms with CHAI or your
  counsel.
